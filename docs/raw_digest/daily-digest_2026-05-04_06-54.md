Benchmarks show 40% faster task completion on complex simulations vs. prior versions. Includes new paper on emergent behaviors. [Source: GitHub Repo](https://github.com/microsoft/autogen), [arXiv: Emergent Coordination in Multi-Agent LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01234), [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=4567890)

[Source: GitHub](https://github.com/trailofbits/aisheild), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/05/04/aisheild_prompt_injection_tool/)
